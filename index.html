I built the Pretorius website as a single file, index.html. It works with no other files, so it’s ready to upload to any host. You can also view it here: https://claude.ai/artifact/8X4RCmV9yJsCjpyM4bxGQ1 (only you can see it until you share it).

The page has a header with the logo, which I cropped from the business card. Below that is a teal banner with “Paarl & all surrounding areas” and a tap-to-call button. Modern Web Guidance

  • accordion: I built each service as a native <details> dropdown and replaced the default triangle with a chevron placed just before the name, not at the far edge of the box. The opening slide animation is turned off for people who’ve set their device to reduce motion.
